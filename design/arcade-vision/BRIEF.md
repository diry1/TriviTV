# TriviTV arcade visual direction — concept v1

Requested 2026-10-21. Canva concept generation covers all four modes, main menu and phone/TV joining. This is a design exploration, not a production UI change.

Direction: expressive 2000s console/arcade graphics with tactile toy forms. Reference moods: Crash (chunky exaggerated shapes), Crazy Taxi (energetic diagonals and display lettering), Fall Guys (colorful dimensional play spaces), Fortnite (confident readable game controls). Original identity, characters and artwork.

Shared language: navy/cream foundation with mango, teal, coral and lavender; thick outlines, offset shadows, modest bevels, large Cyrillic-capable typography, clear remote focus and simple snappy motion. Calm, readable surfaces behind long questions. Safe margins and no scrolling at TV size.

Concept pages: art direction; four-mode main menu; Trivia question and reveal; Millionaire with visible 15-step ladder; Imposter shared TV discussion and private phone role; shared 5x5 Turf board; room QR and phone controller; component states and player palette.

Keep all rules and question bank unchanged. Gameplay correctness and visual concept review are separate; no deployment or APK rebuild is authorized by this design exploration alone.
