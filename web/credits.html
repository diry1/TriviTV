Changes made by TriviTV

We normalize whitespace and HTML entities, retain four source-supplied choices, merge exact duplicate questions, exclude malformed/conflicting or unsuitable records, normalize category labels and apply conservative automatic regional cues. Difficulty ratings were not supplied and have not been invented. Each retained question records its source file, record number and revision.

Community content

Imported questions have passed structural checks, but have not been individually fact-checked. Source wording, correctness and historical references may need editorial review. Region tags are automatic cues, not reviewed cultural targeting; unclassified questions are shared between Europe and US. All questions are currently unrated for difficulty.

Category selection

The bank uses OpenTriviaQA’s topic groups. “Newest” and “rated” are collections, not additional subjects; their unique questions fall under General Knowledge. Source category references remain attached to every imported record.
