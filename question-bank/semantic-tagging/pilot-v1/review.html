50 questions · all 20 categories · 296 assignments · 48 linked Ukrainian drafts.

Two deterministic selections per category, plus ten targeted edge cases. This is a coverage/calibration sample, not a prevalence estimate or independent accuracy benchmark. Correct markers preserve original answer keys; they are not factual approval. Proposals are not active tags. Question-ID links open the local dashboard.
