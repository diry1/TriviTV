# Future question-selection tuning

Recorded 2026-10-21. Notes only; not applied to the current APK build.

- Tag all long questions so they can be filtered and counted.
- Consider assigning long questions an effective selection difficulty of 4 stars, regardless of their knowledge difficulty, to reduce their frequency in the weighted draw.
- Keep knowledge difficulty, text length and effective selection bucket separate in metadata. A length penalty is not evidence of specialist knowledge.
- The character/word threshold for “long” is not decided yet. Include answer-option length when assessing overall reading burden if useful, without silently broadening this request into a blanket exclusion.
- Reassess actual frequency: a 4-star bucket makes these questions less common than easy/medium questions, but its share still rises from 10% to 20% through the game.
