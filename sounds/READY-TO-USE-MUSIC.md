## Attribution

Artist and title alone are not the complete CC BY credit. Include artist, title/source link, license/version/link and any editing (trim, fade, looping, mixing). Example for the explicitly verified 4.0 track:

> “Ethereal Pulse” — Surf House Productions. https://surf-house-productions.bandcamp.com . Source: https://www.free-stock-music.com/surf-house-productions-ethereal-pulse.html . CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Changes: [describe actual edits, or none].

Put this in the game's Music Credits and bundled attribution file. A brief now-playing artist/title display can supplement those credits.

CC BY 4.0 allows copying and adaptations, including commercial use: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Prefer CC0 or CC BY for this project. “Free download” or “royalty free” alone does not establish permission to bundle a recording in an APK.

SoundCloud counterexample: [Come With Me — Unwritten Stories](https://soundcloud.com/unwritten-stories/come-with-me-creative-commons-free-deep-house-music) is **cc-by-sa**, not plain BY. It needs separate ShareAlike review. Avoid NC/ND tracks for the straightforward attribution-only shortlist.
